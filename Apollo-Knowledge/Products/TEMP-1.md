Description: The TEMP-1 is a temperature sensor for ESPHome and Home Assistant over Wi-Fi, with Bluetooth too. It comes as a USB-C powered model or a battery model (rechargeable battery not included). For the most accurate readings it is designed to be used with optional probes through a 3.5mm jack. An onboard AHT20-F measures ambient temperature and humidity and can be affected by heat from the ESP; sleep settings help. An RGB LED and buzzer give alerts.

Features:
- ESP32-based platform, local, private, open source
- Onboard AHT20-F temperature and humidity sensor
- 3.5mm jack for optional probes: 1.5 m flat waterproof DS18B20 probe, 20 cm flat waterproof DS18B20 probe, 1 m stainless steel food-safe NTC probe (not dishwasher safe); multi-probe splitter (3.5mm 4-way) available
- RGB LED and buzzer
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
- Use cases: fridge/freezer, cooking, fish tank, pool/hot tub, attic fan, HVAC, greenhouse, remote monitoring, overheat alerts
- Optional magnet mount
