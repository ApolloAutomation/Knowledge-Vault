Description: The Apollo CAST PRO-1 adds a 3.5mm Line In jack to the casting idea of [[CAST-1]], so a record player, CD player or TV becomes a source in Music Assistant. For output it connects to speakers, soundbars, amplifiers and AV receivers over analog and optical and joins Home Assistant and Music Assistant via Sendspin. It has built-in Ethernet with Wi-Fi as a backup. The page lists it as preorder.

Features:
- Audio in: 3.5mm Line In for line-level sources
- Audio out: 3.5mm dual analog and optical jack, selected in Home Assistant; analog uses a PCM5122 DAC
- Built-in 10/100 Ethernet (data only, no PoE) and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi
- Open source ESPHome firmware with OTA updates; Sendspin support; syncs with other CAST PRO-1 and CAST-1 units
- Bluetooth proxy; Bluetooth LE also used for Improv Wi-Fi setup
- Onboard RGB LEDs (4) plus screw terminal (5V, GND, IO1) for addressable LED strips
- Works with WiZ WizMote remotes, every button configurable in Home Assistant
- Use cases on site: soundbar or AV receiver in whole-home audio, record player in every room, media cabinet over Ethernet, announcements, music-reactive lighting
