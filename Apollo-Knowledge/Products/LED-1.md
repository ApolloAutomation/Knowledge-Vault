# LED-1

## Description

The Apollo LED-1 is an LED controller for advanced lighting projects, pre-flashed with WLED for Wi-Fi control of LED strips and devices. It can be powered over USB-C or through its WAGO power input, and it has two WAGO LED outputs, so it can drive two LED devices or strips independently. It has a built-in replaceable fuse and a digital microphone for audio reactive effects.

## Features

- Pre-flashed with WLED; supports OTA updates through WLED
- Can be powered over USB-C or through one WAGO power input
- Two WAGO LED outputs for independent LED devices or zones
- Replaceable built-in fuse for overload protection
- Integrated digital microphone for audio reactive lighting
- Supports RGB, RGBW and addressable LEDs

## Common questions and answers

### Can the LED-1 control two LED strips separately?

Yes. It has two WAGO LED outputs, so it can drive two LED devices or strips independently.

### How do I power the LED-1?

Over USB-C, or through its single WAGO power input. The other two WAGO connectors are LED outputs.

### What kind of LEDs does the LED-1 support?

RGB, RGBW and addressable LEDs.

### What firmware does the LED-1 come with, and how do I update it?

It comes pre-flashed with WLED for Wi-Fi control, and supports over-the-air (OTA) updates through WLED.

### Does the LED-1 have overload protection?

Yes. It has a built-in fuse for overload protection, and the fuse is replaceable.

### Can the LED-1 react to music?

Yes. It has an integrated digital microphone for audio reactive lighting effects.
