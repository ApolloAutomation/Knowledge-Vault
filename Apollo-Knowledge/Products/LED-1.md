Description: The Apollo LED-1 is an LED controller for advanced lighting projects, pre-flashed with WLED for Wi-Fi control of LED strips and devices. It can be powered over USB-C or through two separate WAGO connectors, so it can power two LED devices or strips independently. It has a built-in replaceable fuse and a digital microphone for audio reactive effects. The page lists it as sold out.

Features:
- Pre-flashed with WLED; supports OTA updates through WLED
- Can be powered over USB-C or through two WAGO connectors
- Two separate outputs for independent LED devices or zones
- Replaceable built-in fuse for overload protection
- Integrated digital microphone for audio reactive lighting
- Supports RGB, RGBW and addressable LEDs
