Description: The R PRO-1 is Apollo's most advanced presence sensor: a compact PoE dual mmWave multisensor for smart homes and commercial buildings. It supports true PoE (802.3 compliant) and USB-C power, with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. It carries the Made for ESPHome badge, ships with Wi-Fi firmware that can be switched to PoE in the device dashboard, and is fully local, open source and cloud-free. The LD2450 is in the base unit; the LD2412 and SCD40 are optional.

Features:
- LD2450 base radar: multi-zone motion tracking with defined zones in its field of view
- Optional LD2412 radar working alongside the LD2450: excels at still presence (seated or resting people); together they give hybrid presence data
- PoE (802.3) or USB-C power
- Built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (Bluetooth tracking and Improv Wi-Fi provisioning)
- LTR390 for ambient lux and UV
- Two addressable RGB LEDs and a piezo buzzer
- Optional SCD40 NDIR CO2 on a mezzanine expansion board; rear mezzanine port for modular add-ons
- Flat-surface, single gang box or ceiling mounting
- Site note: heat can affect the optional temperature/humidity readings (significant offsets needed) and CO2 may be skewed when recessed in walls or ceilings
- Open source code and 3D files
