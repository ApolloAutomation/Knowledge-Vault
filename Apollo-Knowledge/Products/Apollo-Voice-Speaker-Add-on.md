# Apollo-Voice-Speaker-Add-on

## Description

The Apollo Voice Speaker Add-on is a speaker dock for the Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition (Voice PE). It adds a speaker and built-in amplifier for music, announcements and voice responses. The Voice PE sits on top and connects through built-in audio and power cables, with one power cord for both. It ships without a speaker grille. Grilles will be sold separately, and you can also 3D print and add your own.

## Features

- Built-in amplifier and speaker
- Speaker wire header to connect your own speaker
- Built-in audio and power cables for the Voice PE; single power cord powers dock and Voice PE
- Works with Music Assistant and Sendspin for synced multi-room playback
- Use cases: whole-home synced music, audible voice assistant, alerts in every room, bedroom and kids' rooms, local alternative to Alexa or Google

## Common questions and answers

### Does the Voice Speaker Add-on come with the Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition?

No. The Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition (Voice PE) is a separate product. You can add it as an option when ordering. The box contains the Apollo Voice Speaker Add-on.

### Do I need two power supplies for the speaker dock and the Voice PE?

No. The Voice PE sits on top of the dock and connects through built-in audio and power cables, so a single power cord powers both the dock and the Voice PE.

### Can I connect my own speaker to the Voice Speaker Add-on?

Yes. It has a speaker wire header so you can connect your own speaker.

### Can I use the Voice Speaker Add-on for multi-room music?

Yes. It works with Music Assistant and Sendspin for synced multi-room playback.

### Does the Voice Speaker Add-on come with a speaker grille?

No. It ships without a speaker grille. Grilles will be sold separately, and you can also 3D print and add your own.
