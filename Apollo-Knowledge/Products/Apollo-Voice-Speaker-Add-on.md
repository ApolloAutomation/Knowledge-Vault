Description: The Apollo Voice Speaker Add-on is a speaker dock for the Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition (Voice PE). It adds a speaker and built-in amplifier for music, announcements and voice responses. The Voice PE sits on top and connects through built-in audio and power cables, with one power cord for both. The site notes the speaker grid/mesh design is still being tested and asks for feedback on the Apollo Discord. The page lists it as sold out.

Features:
- Built-in amplifier and speaker
- Speaker wire header to connect your own speaker
- Built-in audio and power cables for the Voice PE; single power cord powers dock and Voice PE
- Works with Music Assistant and Sendspin for synced multi-room playback
- Use cases on site: whole-home synced music, audible voice assistant, alerts in every room, bedroom and kids' rooms, local alternative to Alexa or Google
