Description: The Apollo CAST-1 makes non-smart speakers smart. It plugs into powered speakers, amplifiers and AV receivers with a 3.5mm cable and connects them to ESPHome and Music Assistant via Sendspin. You can stream music, announcements and alarms to existing speakers and sync playback across several CAST-1 units or other Sendspin speakers. It connects over Wi-Fi or with the optional Ethernet HAT. The page lists it as preorder.

Features:
- Runs open source ESPHome firmware; supports Sendspin and the ESPHome media player
- 3.5mm analog stereo line out via a TI PCM5122 DAC
- Onboard RGB LED
- Acts as a Bluetooth proxy
- Solder pads for 5 V, GND and IO1 to connect an addressable RGB LED strip or external sensors
- Discovered automatically in Music Assistant and Home Assistant
- Fully local, no cloud required
- Use cases on site: vintage hi-fi revival, budget whole-home audio, Home Assistant announcements, garage/workshop/patio audio, reactive lighting
