Description: The Official ESPHome C6 Board is the compact ESP32-C6 board at the heart of the [[ESK-1]] ESPHome Starter Kit, sold on its own. It is built around the Espressif ESP32-C6-MINI-1 and brings Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE and Thread on one chip (Zigbee is possible at chip level for advanced users). It is powered over USB-C or an optional rechargeable LiPo battery and is set up in the ESPHome Device Builder with no code required. Everything runs locally with no cloud, subscriptions or accounts.

Features:
- Connect up to two modules through its 14-pin FPC ports; two modules can run at once to make a multisensor
- Works with the Starter Kit modules: motion, temperature and humidity, button, and ESPHome Casita
- Onboard RGB status LED
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE or Thread connection to Home Assistant
- Can be deployed cable-free on a rechargeable LiPo battery (battery sold separately)
- Open PCB-based design; most of the profit supports the Open Home Foundation
- Configured with ESPHome Device Builder (Mac, Windows, Linux) or the ESPHome Home Assistant app
