# M-1

## Description

The M-1 LED Matrix is a display with 4096 pixels in a HUB75 format, driven by Apollo's own 5V controller (powered over USB-C or WAGO), so no additional power source is needed. The compact controller hides behind the matrix for a clean install. It comes pre-flashed with WLED firmware; you can customize animations, effects and colors, or use the Pixel Magic tool to make your own images. The M-1 is designed as a complete product, with the controller made specifically for the HUB75 panel Apollo sells. It can be used with other HUB75 panels, but you may hit compatibility issues.

## Features

- 4096 pixels, HUB75 format
- Built-in Apollo LED controller with WLED pre-installed (WLED-MM is credited)
- Controller hides behind the panel; compact form factor for wall or panel mounting
- Pixel Magic tool for custom images and designs
- Up to four panels can be connected horizontally; extra power injection is needed for the full effect
- Optional digital microphone for audio-reactive effects (works only with the latest Rev 6 board)
- Use cases: digital signage, interactive art, real-time data visualization (weather, stocks, social feeds), smart home notifications, scoreboards, ambient lighting, retro pixel art and gaming, music visualization, sound-responsive displays, accessibility alerts (doorbells, alarms, baby monitors)

## Common questions and answers

### Do I need a separate power supply for the M-1?

No. The M-1 controller runs on 5V 3A over USB-C or through its WAGO connector, so no additional power source is needed for a single panel. If you chain several panels, extra power injection is needed for the full effect.

### How many M-1 panels can I connect together?

Up to four panels can be connected horizontally. Extra power injection is needed for the full effect. For each extra panel you need the panel, a data cable and a power module; the extra panel kits include these plus a stand and USB-C cable. The 4 Panel Kits support 1x4 and 2x2 setups.

### Why doesn't the microphone work on my M-1?

The optional M-1-Digital-Microphone for audio-reactive effects works only with the latest Rev 6 controller board.

### Can I use the M-1 controller with a different HUB75 panel?

The M-1 controller is made specifically for the HUB75 panel Apollo sells. It can be used with other HUB75 panels, but you may hit compatibility issues.

### What firmware does the M-1 run?

It comes pre-flashed with WLED (WLED-MM is credited), running on the [[ESP32-S3]] in the controller. You can customize animations, effects and colors in WLED.

### How do I put my own images on the M-1?

Use the Pixel Magic tool to make custom images and designs for the matrix.
