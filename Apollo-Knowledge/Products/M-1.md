Description: The M-1 LED Matrix is a display with 4096 pixels in a HUB75 format, driven by Apollo's own 5V (USB-C or WAGO) controller, so no additional power source is needed. The compact controller hides behind the matrix for a clean install. It comes pre-flashed with WLED firmware; you can customize animations, effects, and colors, or use the Pixel Magic tool to make your own images. The M-1 is designed as a complete product, with the controller made specifically for the HUB75 panel Apollo sells; it can be used with other HUB75 panels but you may hit compatibility issues.

Features:
- 4096 pixels, HUB75 format
- Built-in Apollo LED controller with WLED pre-installed (WLED-MM credited on the site)
- Controller hides behind the panel; compact form factor for wall or panel mounting
- Pixel Magic tool for custom images and designs
- Up to four panels can be connected horizontally; extra power injection is needed for the full effect
- Optional digital microphone for audio-reactive effects (works only with the latest Rev 6 board)
- Use cases: digital signage, interactive art, real-time data visualization (weather, stocks, social feeds), smart home notifications, scoreboards, ambient lighting, retro pixel art and gaming, music visualization, sound-responsive displays, accessibility alerts (doorbells, alarms, baby monitors)
