# PLT-1

## Description

The PLT-1 is an indoor plant multisensor that measures soil moisture, air temperature, humidity, LUX and UV, with an onboard piezo buzzer and addressable RGB LED. It uses a capacitive soil moisture sensor, and the ground-contact portion is protected by a conformal coating for longevity. Soil temperature can be measured with an optional probe ([[Short-Temperature-Probe]] or [[Long-Temperature-Probe]]). It comes in a battery (B) version and a non-battery version; the non-battery version is significantly smaller. The battery version does not include a battery or charger.

## Features

- Capacitive soil moisture sensor with conformal coating
- AHT20-F air temperature and humidity sensor (dust-protected)
- LTR390 UV and LUX sensor
- Optional DS18B20 waterproof soil temperature probe: short flat cable 20 cm ([[Short-Temperature-Probe]]) or long flat cable 1.5 m ([[Long-Temperature-Probe]])
- RGB LED and piezo buzzer for alerts
- Battery and non-battery versions
- Home Assistant integration
- Use cases: automated watering, sunlight optimization, temperature and humidity management, soil temperature monitoring, visual and audio alerts

## Common questions and answers

### Does the PLT-1 battery version come with a battery?

No. The battery (B) version does not include a battery or a charger. You need to supply a rechargeable flat top 18650 battery and a charger yourself.

### What battery does the PLT-1 use?

A rechargeable flat top 18650 battery, in the battery version only. The non-battery version does not take a battery.

### How long does the PLT-1 battery last?

An estimated 6 months with a quality rechargeable 18650 and the default sleep/wake settings.

### What is the difference between the battery and non-battery PLT-1?

The battery version runs on a rechargeable 18650 (not included). The non-battery version has no battery and is significantly smaller.

### Can the PLT-1 measure soil temperature?

Yes, with an optional DS18B20 waterproof probe: the 20 cm [[Short-Temperature-Probe]] or the 1.5 m [[Long-Temperature-Probe]].

### Is the PLT-1 waterproof?

No waterproof rating is given for the sensor body, so please contact support before exposing it to water. The part that goes into the soil has a conformal coating for longevity, and the optional soil temperature probes are waterproof.

### Can the PLT-1 water my plants automatically?

The PLT-1 measures soil moisture. Pair it with the [[PUMP-1]] fluid pump, which is built to work with the PLT-1 to automate watering.
