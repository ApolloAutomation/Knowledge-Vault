Description: The PLT-1 is an indoor plant multisensor measuring soil moisture, air temperature, humidity, LUX and UV, with an onboard piezo buzzer and addressable RGB LED. It uses a capacitive soil moisture sensor, with the ground contact portion protected by a conformal coating for longevity. Soil temperature can be measured with an optional probe. It comes in a battery (B) and non-battery version; the non-battery version is significantly smaller. The battery version does not include a battery or charger.

Features:
- Capacitive soil moisture sensor with conformal coating
- AHT20-F air temperature and humidity sensor (dust-protected)
- LTR390 UV and LUX sensor
- Optional DS18B20 waterproof soil temperature probe (short flat cable 20 cm, or long flat cable 1.5 m)
- RGB LED and piezo buzzer for alerts
- Battery and non-battery versions; Home Assistant integration
- Use cases: automated watering, sunlight optimization, temperature and humidity management, soil temperature monitoring, visual and audio alerts
