Description: The MTR-1 is a multi-target radar multisensor for ESPHome that packs a diverse array of sensors into an affordable, compact package. It can measure multiple targets, zones, target coordinates, RGB, buzzer, LUX/UV and optional CO2. It is powered via USB-C and a standard wall plug, and uses Wi-Fi to connect to ESPHome on a Home Assistant install. Software and CAD drawings are offered to the community.

Features:
- Multi-target, multi-zone mmWave radar (HLK-LD2450) that reports where people are, not just that someone is present (e.g. at the stove, sink, couch or bed)
- LUX and UV sensing (LTR-390UV)
- Optional CO2 sensor (SCD-40): pre-calibrated and ready to use; not meeting CO2 safety monitoring requirements per NBIC, NFPA or IFC
- Bluetooth tracker via the ESP32-C3-Mini (YAML config required)
- RGB pixel (RGB neopixel)
- Piezo buzzer with different beep patterns for different alerts
- Compact size, USB-C powered, Wi-Fi to ESPHome
- Back male USB-C adapter (note on site: some wall plugs and receptacles with smart charging do not work)
- Open software and CAD files; Discord community
