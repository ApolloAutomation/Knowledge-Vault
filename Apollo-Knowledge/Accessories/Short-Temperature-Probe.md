# Short-Temperature-Probe

## Description

A 20 cm / 7.8 inch waterproof DS18B20 temperature probe for the [[PLT-1]] to monitor soil temperature. It also works with the [[TEMP-1]].

## Features

- 20 cm / 7.8 inches long
- Waterproof flat cable, submersible
- Intended for monitoring soil temperature with the PLT-1
- Also works with the TEMP-1
- Works with the TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter (listed as a Dallas short probe)

## Specs

- Probe type: [[DS18B20]] waterproof flat cable (submersible)
- Probe length: 20 cm / 7.8 in
- Max temperature: 85 °C / 185 °F

## Compatible with

- [[PLT-1]]
- [[TEMP-1]]
- TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter

## Common questions and answers

### How do I measure soil temperature with the PLT-1?

Add the short temperature probe. It is a 20 cm / 7.8 in waterproof DS18B20 probe intended for monitoring soil temperature with the [[PLT-1]].

### Does the short probe work with the TEMP-1?

Yes. It works with both the [[PLT-1]] and the [[TEMP-1]].

### What is the maximum temperature the short probe can measure?

85 °C / 185 °F.

### Can I use the short probe with the multi-probe splitter?

Yes. It works with the TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter, where it is listed as the Dallas short probe.

### What is the difference between the short and long probe?

Length. The short probe is 20 cm / 7.8 in; the [[Long-Temperature-Probe]] is 1.5 m / 59 in. Both are waterproof DS18B20 flat-cable probes rated to 85 °C / 185 °F.
