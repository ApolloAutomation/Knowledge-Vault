# TEMP-1-Food-Probe

## Description

A 1 m (~3 ft) stainless steel, food-safe probe with an NTC sensor, sold for the [[TEMP-1]].

## Features

- Stainless steel, food-safe probe with NTC sensor
- 1 m (~3 ft) long
- Not dishwasher safe
- Not compatible with the TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter

## Specs

- Probe type: NTC sensor, stainless steel, food-safe
- Probe length: 1 m (~3 ft)
- Max temperature: 204 °C / 400 °F

## Compatible with

- [[TEMP-1]] (sold as the TEMP-1 Food Probe)
- Not compatible with the TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter
