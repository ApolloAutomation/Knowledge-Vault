Description: A 90-degree male-to-male USB-C outlet mount for the MTR-1 and MSR-2. It allows a clean installation without a cable. The site says it might be the smallest right-angle adapter on the market and that it can work for other applications.

Features:
- Right-angle (90-degree) male-to-male USB-C
- Outlet mount, no cable needed
- Optional USA wall charger add-on in a drop-down

Compatible with: [[MTR-1]], [[MSR-2]]

Included/variants:
- Mount only, or with USA wall charger ([[Front-Facing-USB-C-Wall-Plug]]). The site lists the bundle as "90-Degree USB-C Mount With US Plug" (handle mtr-1-outlet-mount-with-us-plug, no description), folded here.
