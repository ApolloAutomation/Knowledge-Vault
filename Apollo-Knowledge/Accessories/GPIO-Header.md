Description: A header giving access to 3V, 5V, GND, I2C and 6 GPIO. It works with all Apollo sensors via the mezzanine port, and sensors can be powered from the 5V GPIO pin. Optional dupont cables are in the drop-down. The site links a how-to for controlling an LED strip with it. Ozone and mmWave sensor are not included.

Features:
- Exposes 3V, 5V, GND, I2C and 6 GPIO
- Attaches through the mezzanine port
- Sensor can be powered from the 5V GPIO pin
- Optional [[Dupont-Cables]] (about 20 male to male and about 20 male to female, about 40 total)
- Example use: controlling an LED strip

Compatible with: [[AIR-1]], [[MSR-2]], [[MTR-1]] (sensor drop-down)

Included/variants: Header only, or with dupont cables (bundle "GPIO Header With Dupont Cables", folded here). Variants by sensor: AIR-1, MSR-2, MTR-1.
