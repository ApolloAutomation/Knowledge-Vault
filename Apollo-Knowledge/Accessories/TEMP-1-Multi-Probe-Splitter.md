Description: A 3.5mm aux 4-way splitter that lets a [[TEMP-1]] or TEMP-1B read several temperature probes at once. Needs firmware 25.11.20.1 or newer.

Features:
- 3.5mm male to 4 x 3.5mm female adapter
- For TEMP-1 and TEMP-1B on firmware 25.11.20.1 or newer
- Works with the Apollo Dallas short and long probes: [[Short-Temperature-Probe]], [[Long-Temperature-Probe]]
- Not compatible with the [[TEMP-1-Food-Probe]]
- The 3rd and 4th probes are disabled by default and must be enabled in the firmware
- Use cases on site: multiple temperature probes, fridge/freezer monitoring with one TEMP-1, attic and other ambient temperatures, multiple aquariums or other water sources
- Guide: https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/temp1/addons/multiple-probes/

Compatible with: [[TEMP-1]], TEMP-1B

Included/variants: Single item, no variants. What's in the box: 3.5mm male to 4 x 3.5mm female adapter.
