Description: A breakout board for easy integration of the high-accuracy [[SCD40]] CO2 sensor. It has two STEMMA QT connectors and standard header holes, which simplifies prototyping. It suits smart home, environmental monitoring, and IoT projects, and is compatible with Apollo's DEV-1 and DEV-2 boards. The site says it gives CO2 readings with integrated temperature and humidity compensation.

Features:
- Two STEMMA QT connectors (plug and play)
- Standard header holes for soldering or breadboards
- Compact, lightweight form factor
- Use cases: indoor air quality, ventilation/HVAC automation, greenhouse and agriculture, environmental research, education

Compatible with: [[SCD40]], DEV-1 (ESP32-C3) and DEV-2 (ESP32-C6) development boards

Included/variants: Box has the breakout board and a sticker. Add-ons: Dupont cables, breadboard, USB-C cable (1.5m/5ft), front facing USB-C charger, DEV-1 ESP32-C3 development board, DEV-2 ESP32-C6 development board.
