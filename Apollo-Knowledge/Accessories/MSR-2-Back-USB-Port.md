Description: A female USB-C port that attaches to the back of the MSR-2. It lets you plug a USB-C cable in from the back, hiding the cables for a cleaner installation. The page links an install video.

Features:
- Female USB-C port on the back of the MSR-2
- Hides cables for a cleaner installation
- Install video linked on the product page: https://youtu.be/hkNrhJuMmxI?t=137

Compatible with: [[MSR-2]]

Included/variants: Sold alone, and also as the bundle "MSR-2 Male and Female Back USB-C Port" (folded into this note; the bundle page has no description, so its exact contents are Not stated on site; see also [[MSR-2-Outlet-Mount]]).
