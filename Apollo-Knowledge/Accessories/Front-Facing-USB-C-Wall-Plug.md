Description: A front facing female USB-C wall plug (USA plug only), used to power Apollo sensors. The page states 20W 5V 3A for the US plug.

Features:
- Front facing female USB-C port
- USA plug only
- Used to power your sensors
- Optional USB-C cable add-on

Compatible with: [[MSR-2]], [[MTR-1]] (pairs with [[90-Degree-USB-C-Mount]], [[MSR-2-Outlet-Mount]]); generally powers any Apollo sensor

Included/variants: Plug only, or with a [[USB-C-Cable]]. The bundle "Front Facing USB-C Wall Plug (USA Plug) and Cable" (handle charger-and-cable) lists a 1.5m USB-C male-to-male cable and the front facing wall charger (US plug 20W 5V 3A); folded here.
