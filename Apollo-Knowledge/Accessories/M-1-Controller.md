Description: The M-1 LED Matrix controller on its own, without a panel. It is designed to plug into the HUB75 panel Apollo sells, but can work with most other panels using a male-to-female data cable. The site credits the WLED and WLED-MM teams.

Features:
- Controller only; HUB75 panel not included
- Plugs directly into the Apollo HUB75 panel
- Works with most other HUB75 panels via a male-to-female data cable

Compatible with: [[M-1]], [[HUB75-Matrix-Panel]]

Included/variants: Optional add-ons on the page: [[M-1-Data-Cable]] (multi-panel setups or other HUB75 panels), [[M-1-Power-Module]] (power a HUB75 panel over USB-C), USB-C cable (about 3m / 5.5ft, male to male), top facing dual USB-C wall charger (USA style).
