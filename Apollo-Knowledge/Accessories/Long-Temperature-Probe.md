# Long-Temperature-Probe

## Description

A 1.5 m / 59 inch waterproof DS18B20 temperature probe for the [[TEMP-1]]. It also works with the [[PLT-1]].

## Features

- 1.5 m / 59 inches long
- Waterproof flat cable, submersible
- Works with the TEMP-1 and the PLT-1
- Works with the TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter (listed as a Dallas long probe)

## Specs

- Probe type: [[DS18B20]] waterproof flat cable (submersible)
- Probe length: 1.5 m / 59 in
- Max temperature: 85 °C / 185 °F

## Compatible with

- [[PLT-1]]
- TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter

## Common questions and answers

### Can the long temperature probe go in water?

Yes. It is a waterproof flat-cable DS18B20 probe and is submersible.

### What is the maximum temperature the long probe can measure?

85 °C / 185 °F.

### Does the long probe work with the PLT-1?

Yes. It works with both the [[TEMP-1]] and the [[PLT-1]].

### Can I use the long probe with the multi-probe splitter?

Yes. It works with the TEMP-1-Multi-Probe-Splitter, where it is listed as the Dallas long probe.

### What is the difference between the long and short probe?

Length. The long probe is 1.5 m / 59 in; the [[Short-Temperature-Probe]] is 20 cm / 7.8 in. Both are waterproof DS18B20 flat-cable probes rated to 85 °C / 185 °F.
