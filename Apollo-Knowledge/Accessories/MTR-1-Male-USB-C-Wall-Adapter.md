Description: A male USB-C plug that mounts onto the MTR-1's top mezzanine port, so the MTR-1 can plug straight into a USB-C wall plug. The wall plug is not included. The site warns that some wall plugs and receptacles with smart charging do not work.

Features:
- Mounts on the MTR-1 top mezzanine port
- Plugs straight into a USB-C wall plug
- Wall plug not included
- Some wall plugs and receptacles with smart charging do not work

Compatible with: [[MTR-1]]

Included/variants: Adapter only. A bundle "MTR-1 Outlet Mount With US Plug" (listed on the site as "90-Degree USB-C Mount With US Plug") is folded into [[90-Degree-USB-C-Mount]]. Wall plug option: [[Front-Facing-USB-C-Wall-Plug]].
