# PUMP-1-Tubing

## Description

Two 24 in pieces of flexible silicone tubing (48 in total) for the [[PUMP-1]], one for inflow and one for outflow. Also sold inside the PUMP-1-Full-Accessory-Kit.

## Features

- Two 24 in pieces (48 in total)
- Flexible silicone tubing
- Add-on listing: includes two pieces, for inflow and outflow

## Specs

- Material: Flexible silicone
- Length: Two 24 in pieces (48 in total)
- Inner diameter: 6 mm
- Outer diameter: 10 mm

## Compatible with

## What's in the box

Two 24 in pieces of silicone tubing. Single item, no variants. Also included in the PUMP-1-Full-Accessory-Kit.

## Common questions and answers

### What size tubing does the PUMP-1 use?

The PUMP-1 tubing is flexible silicone with a 6 mm inner diameter and a 10 mm outer diameter.

### How much tubing do I get?

Two 24 in pieces, 48 in in total: one for inflow and one for outflow.

### Is tubing included with the PUMP-1?

No. The [[PUMP-1]] box contains the pump and an Apollo sticker. Tubing is sold separately or as part of the PUMP-1-Full-Accessory-Kit.
