Description: A breakout board with two STEMMA QT connectors and header holes for connecting the [[SEN55]] environmental sensor and the [[SCD40]] CO2 sensor. It is built for rapid prototyping and smart home use and is compatible with Apollo's DEV-1 and DEV-2 boards. The site says it supports temperature, humidity, and VOCs (SEN55) and CO2 (SCD40).

Features:
- Two STEMMA QT connectors
- Standard header holes for soldering or breadboards
- Compact, lightweight form factor
- Use cases: indoor air quality, HVAC and purifier automation, prototyping, greenhouse monitoring, education

Compatible with: [[SEN55]], [[SCD40]], DEV-1 (ESP32-C3) and DEV-2 (ESP32-C6) development boards

Included/variants: Two variants: No SCD40 CO2 sensor, or With SCD40 CO2 sensor. Box has the breakout board and a sticker. Add-ons: Dupont cables, breadboard, USB-C cable (1.5m/5ft), front facing USB-C charger, DEV-1 and DEV-2 boards.
