Description: An outlet mount for the MSR-2. It is a male to male USB-C connector that plugs into a wall plug. It is sized for the optional wall plug but can work with any USB-C plug.

Features:
- Male to male USB-C connector
- Plugs directly into a wall plug (no cable needed)
- Sized for the optional Apollo wall plug, works with any USB-C plug

Compatible with: [[MSR-2]]

Included/variants:
- Mount only, or with the USA wall charger option (the site also lists this as a bundle "MSR-2 Outlet Mount With US Plug", folded into this note). The plug is [[Front-Facing-USB-C-Wall-Plug]].
- Related: [[90-Degree-USB-C-Mount]] (also fits MSR-2)
