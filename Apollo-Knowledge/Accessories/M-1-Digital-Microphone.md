Description: A compact digital microphone module that turns the M-1 LED Matrix into an audio-reactive display, with visualizations that respond to music, voice, or ambient sound. It only works with the latest Rev 6 board. The site describes it as a digital MEMS microphone with low-latency processing, compatible with WLED-MM audio-reactive effects, powered directly through the M-1 controller connection. An install guide is linked on the Apollo wiki: https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/m1/addons/adding-microphone-to-m-1/

Features:
- Audio-reactive effects on the M-1
- Use cases: music visualization, party displays, gaming audio feedback, ambient soundscapes, baby monitor or doorbell visual alerts, voice assistant feedback, streaming studio level monitor, pet bark detection, appliance alerts, accessibility alerts

Compatible with: [[M-1]] (Rev 6 controller board only)

Included/variants: Box contains the digital microphone. Also sold as an add-on on the M-1 page.
