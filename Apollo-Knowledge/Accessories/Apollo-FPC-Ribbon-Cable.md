Description: The 14-pin flexible printed circuit (FPC) ribbon cable that connects modules to the ESPHome C6 Board. It is the same cable that ships with the ESPHome Starter Kit, sold as a spare, replacement, or extra. The zero insertion force connector slides in with the blue side up and latches, with no soldering or tools. Power off the board, lift the latch, insert, press down to lock; do not pull on the cable itself.

Features:
- 14-pin FPC ribbon cable, ZIF latching connector
- Works with either 14-pin FPC port on the ESPHome C6 Board (top or bottom)
- Use cases: run a second module, replace a lost cable, attach a module or the breakout, keep a spare

Compatible with: [[Apollo-Breakout-Module]], the ESPHome C6 Board, all Apollo Starter Kit modules

Included/variants: Box lists 3x Apollo FPC Cable.
