Description: The PN7160 is the NXP NFC controller at the heart of the NFC-1. It lets the NFC-1 read, write, clean and format NFC tags, and supports NTAG / MIFARE Ultralight and MIFARE Classic tags. It also enables Card Emulation, so the NFC-1 acts as a tag that a phone can tap to open a link or launch the Home Assistant app on Android.
Used in: [[NFC-1]]

Source: NXP PN7160_PN7161 data sheet (Rev. 4.2, 22 July 2026)
Ambient operating temperature: -30 to +85 °C
Output power: 1.3 W transmitter output
Supported tags and modes: ISO 14443 A/B, ISO 15693, MIFARE 1K/4K, MIFARE DESFire, FeliCa and NFC Forum tags 1–5; can also act as a card with ISO 14443 A/B (T4T); peer-to-peer works only up to firmware 12.50.0A
Operating modes: reader, writer and emulator
