# PN7160

## Description

The PN7160 is the NXP NFC controller at the heart of the [[NFC-1]]. It lets the NFC-1 read, write, clean and format NFC tags, and it supports NTAG / MIFARE Ultralight and MIFARE Classic tags. It also enables Card Emulation, so the NFC-1 acts as a tag that a phone can tap to open a link or launch the Home Assistant app on Android.

## Used in

- [[NFC-1]]

## Specs

- Tag types supported on the NFC-1: NTAG / MIFARE Ultralight and MIFARE Classic
- Functions on the NFC-1: read, write, clean and format tags; Card Emulation

## Datasheet figures (chip manufacturer, not an Apollo rating)

Source: NXP PN7160_PN7161 Near Field Communication (NFC) controller, Product data sheet, Rev. 4.2, 22 July 2026 (https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PN7160_PN7161.pdf)

- Ambient operating temperature: -30 to +85 °C
- Output power: 1.3 W transmitter output
- Reader modes: ISO/IEC 14443 A/B, ISO/IEC 15693, MIFARE 1K/4K, MIFARE DESFire and NFC Forum tags T1T to T5T; FeliCa reader mode only up to firmware 12.50.0A
- Card emulation: T4T over ISO/IEC 14443 A/B
- Peer-to-peer: supported only up to firmware 12.50.0A
