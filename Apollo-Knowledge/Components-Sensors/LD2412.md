Description: HLK-LD2412 mmWave radar sensor. On the R PRO-1 it is an optional add-on that gives high-accuracy still presence detection, ideal for seated or sleeping individuals, and it works in addition to the [[ld2450]]. It is also sold on its own as the LD2412 mmWave Sensor. The site describes it as a high-performance radar for precise presence and motion detection using millimeter-wave technology; it works in total darkness, in lit environments and through thin obstacles, with high sensitivity and a low false alarm rate. Detection covers presence, motion, direction sensing and basic gesture recognition. Site-listed uses include lighting and HVAC control, occupancy monitoring, security, automated doors/gates, robotics and industrial automation. The box contains the mmWave sensor and an Apollo sticker.
Used in: [[R-PRO-1]] (optional add-on)
