# LD2412

## Description

The HLK-LD2412 is an mmWave radar sensor. On the [[R-PRO-1]] (R PRO-1) it is an optional add-on that gives high-accuracy still presence detection, ideal for seated or sleeping people. It works alongside the R PRO-1's built-in [[LD2450]]. It is also sold on its own as the LD2412 mmWave Sensor.

It is a high-performance radar for precise presence and motion detection using millimeter-wave technology. It works in total darkness, in lit environments and through thin obstacles, with high sensitivity and a low false alarm rate. Detection covers presence, motion, direction sensing and basic gesture recognition. It is suited to still targets, and its moving and still energy can be tuned.

Typical uses include lighting and HVAC control, occupancy monitoring, security, automated doors and gates, robotics and industrial automation.
