Description: Onboard RGB LED used for visual notifications and status feedback. Apollo calls it an RGB neopixel on the MSR-2 and MTR-1, two addressable RGB LEDs on the R PRO-1, and RGB pixels on the AIR-1. The PLT-1 has an addressable RGB LED, and the NFC-1 and TEMP-1 use an RGB LED for feedback and alerts.
