Description: Espressif ESP32-S3 microcontroller, the MCU on the CAST-1 and CAST PRO-1 audio casting devices and listed in the M-1 LED matrix product details. The CAST-1 version has 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM. The M-1 controller ships pre-flashed with WLED.
Used in: [[CAST-1]], [[CAST-PRO-1]], [[M-1]], [[M-1-Controller]]

Operating range: Not stated on site
Wifi capability: 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (CAST-1 and CAST PRO-1)
Can be bluetooth proxy: Yes (CAST-1 is Home Assistant Bluetooth Proxy capable; CAST PRO-1 uses Bluetooth LE for Bluetooth proxy and Improv Wi-Fi setup)
