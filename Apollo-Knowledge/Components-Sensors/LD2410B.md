# LD2410B

## Description

The HLK-LD2410B is an mmWave radar module. On the [[MSR-2]] it provides presence and still-presence detection and tracks 1 target. It has very good still detection and very good tuning capability, and it can be tuned to remove false positives.

The LD2410B reports the distance to a target, not the target's position in the room. The MSR-2 uses that distance to decide which of its three configurable zones is occupied.

## Used in

- [[MSR-2]]

## Specs

- Number of occupants: 1 target tracked at a time
- Distance: Up to about 6 m (19.7 ft) detection range
- Max distance: About 6 m (19.7 ft)
- Max targets: 1 target
- Number of zones: 3 configurable distance-based zones on the MSR-2
- Field of view: Unknown — escalate to support
- Max square footage of coverage given height off ground: Unknown — escalate to support
- Mounting orientation: Unknown — escalate to support
