# MiCS-4514

## Description

The MiCS-4514 is a DFRobot multi-gas sensor. It works with the [[AIR-1]] as an optional add-on and connects with the MiCS-4514 Cable. It detects CO, C2H5OH (ethanol), H2, NO2, NH3 (ammonia) and CH4 (methane).

During beta testing, the methane reading spiked when the sensor was exposed to high amounts of other gases. Apollo recommends treating the MiCS-4514 as an exists/doesn't-exist gas indicator rather than an exact PPM readout.

The MiCS-4514 is not for gas safety monitoring and does not meet NBIC, NFPA or IFC gas safety monitoring requirements.

## Used in

- [[AIR-1]] - optional multi-gas sensor
- MiCS-4514-Cable - required to connect the sensor

## Specs

- Gases detected: CO, ethanol (C2H5OH), H2, NO2, NH3 (ammonia), CH4 (methane)
- Reading type: Best used as a gas present/absent indicator, not an exact PPM readout
- Known behaviour: Methane reading can spike when other gases are present in high amounts
- Safety rating: Not for gas safety monitoring (does not meet NBIC, NFPA or IFC requirements)
- Connection: MiCS-4514 Cable

## Datasheet figures (chip manufacturer, not an Apollo rating)

Source: SGX Sensortech MiCS-4514 Data Sheet, 0278 rev 16 (https://sgxsensortech.com/uploads/f_note/0278_Datasheet-MiCS-4514-rev-16.pdf)

- Detectable gases: CO 1–1000 ppm, NO2 0.05–10 ppm, ethanol 10–500 ppm, H2 1–1000 ppm, NH3 1–500 ppm, CH4 above 1000 ppm
- Supply voltage: 4.9–5.1 V
- Ambient operating temperature: -30 to 85 °C
- Humidity range: 5–95 %RH
