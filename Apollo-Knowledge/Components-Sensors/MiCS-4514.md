Description: The MiCS-4514 is a DFRobot multi-gas sensor that works with the Apollo AIR-1 as an optional add-on and needs the MiCS-4514 Cable. It detects CO, C2H5OH (ethanol), H2, NO2, NH3 and CH4. Beta testers saw the methane reading spike when exposed to high amounts of other gases, so the site suggests using it as an exists/doesn't-exist gas indicator rather than an exact PPM readout. The site says it is not for gas safety monitoring (NBIC, NFPA, IFC) and that it is hard to source with intermittent stock.
Used in: [[AIR-1]], MiCS-4514-Cable

Source: SGX Sensortech MiCS-4514 datasheet (0278 rev 16)
Detectable gases: CO 1–1000 ppm, NO2 0.05–10 ppm, ethanol 10–500 ppm, H2 1–1000 ppm, NH3 1–500 ppm, CH4 above 1000 ppm
Ambient operating temperature: -30 to 85 °C
Humidity range: 5–95 %RH
