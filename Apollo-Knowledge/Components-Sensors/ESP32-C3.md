Description: The ESP32-C3 (named ESP32-C3-Mini on the site) is the main microcontroller in several Apollo devices. DEV-1 uses the ESP32-C3-MINI-1, described as a WiFi and Bluetooth-enabled chip. It also handles Bluetooth tracking on the MSR-2 (YAML config required). The site notes that heat from the ESP32-C3 causes a temperature offset on the AIR-1.
