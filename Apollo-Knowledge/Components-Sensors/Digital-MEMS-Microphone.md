Description: A digital MEMS microphone module for high-quality audio capture. It gives low-latency processing for real-time LED synchronization and is compatible with WLED-MM audio-reactive effects. It is powered directly through the M-1 controller connection and only works with the latest Rev 6 board. Sold as the accessory [[M-1-Digital-Microphone]].
