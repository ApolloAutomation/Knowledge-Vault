Description: Espressif ESP32-C6 microcontroller (sold on Apollo boards as the ESP32-C6-MINI-1 module). It is the MCU that provides Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE, and the site also lists Thread, with Zigbee enabled on the DEV-2. On the Apollo Breakout Module the C6 drives a shared I2C bus on GPIO0 (SCL) and GPIO1 (SDA) and 1-Wire on GPIO6, and powers the connectors from its controlled rail, which is off during sleep. The NFC-1 uses it with 8MB flash and a built-in Bluetooth proxy that can be switched on or off.
Used in: [[DEV-2]], [[ESPHome-C6-Board]], [[ESK-1]], [[NFC-1]], [[BTN-1]], [[Apollo-Breakout-Module]], [[PUMP-1]]

Operating range: -40 C to +85 C (stated for the ESPHome C6 Board; Not stated on site for the other products)
Wifi capability: Wi-Fi (plus Bluetooth LE and Thread; Zigbee enabled on DEV-2)
Can be bluetooth proxy: Yes (NFC-1 has a built-in Bluetooth proxy that can be switched on or off)
