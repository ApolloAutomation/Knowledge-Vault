Description: Air quality sensing module sold as an optional add-on to the ESK-1 Starter Kit (it requires the Breakout Module). It is a sensing platform for particulate matter, relative humidity and temperature, VOC and NOx measurements, and reports up to eight parameters: PM1, PM2.5, PM4, PM10, temperature, relative humidity, VOC Index and NOx Index. Its core is a miniaturized MEMS-based particulate matter sensing component, the SPS6x. The site says it suits IAQ-standard applications such as RESET and WELL Building Standard. The box contains the SEN65 module and a SEN65 cable.
Used in: [[ESK-1]], [[Apollo-Breakout-Module]]

Source: Sensirion SEN6x datasheet (D1 Version 1.0, Oct 2026)
Temperature accuracy: typ ±0.45 °C, max ±0.7 °C (15–30 °C, 50 %RH)
Humidity accuracy: typ ±4.5 %RH (range 0–100 %RH)
PM range: 0–1000 µg/m³ for PM1, PM2.5, PM4 and PM10
PM precision (PM1, PM2.5): ±(5 µg/m³ + 5 % of reading) up to 100 µg/m³, then ±10 %
VOC and NOx index range: 1–500
Operating range: recommended 10–40 °C; absolute short-term max/min -10 to 50 °C
Lifetime: >10 years
