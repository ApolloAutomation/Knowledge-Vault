# LTR390

## Description

The LTR390 (LTR-390UV) is a light sensor that measures ambient lux and UV. The UV reading shows sunlight coming through a window, and the lux reading is used for lighting automations.

## Used in

- [[MSR-2]] - lux and UV sensing
- [[MTR-1]] - lux and UV sensing
- [[R-PRO-1]] - ambient lux and UV sensing
- [[PLT-1]] - UV and lux sensing

## Specs

- Operating temp: Unknown — escalate to support
