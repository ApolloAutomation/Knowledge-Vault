Description: The W5500 is the Ethernet module used in the W5500 Ethernet HAT, an optional add-on for the CAST-1. It gives the CAST-1 a direct wired Ethernet connection instead of Wi-Fi. It carries data only and does not provide PoE.
Used in: [[CAST-1]], [[W5500-Ethernet-HAT]]
