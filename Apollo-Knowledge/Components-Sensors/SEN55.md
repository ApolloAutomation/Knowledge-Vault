Description: The SEN55 is the air quality sensor on the AIR-1, from a reputable manufacturer per the site. It reports PM1, PM2.5, PM4, PM10, VOC, NOx, temperature and humidity. The site describes it as a high-precision environmental sensor. It is offered on breakout boards for DEV-1 and DEV-2 (SEN55 Breakout Board, and SEN55 plus SCD40 Breakout Board).
Used in: [[AIR-1]], [[DEV-1]], [[DEV-2]], SEN55-SCD40-Breakout-Board

Source: Sensirion SEN5x datasheet (Version 2, March 2022; SparkFun-hosted copy). Sensirion lists the SEN55 as not recommended for new designs, with the SEN65 as successor.
Temperature accuracy: typ ±0.45 °C, max ±0.7 °C
Humidity accuracy: typ ±4.5 %RH
PM range: 0–1000 µg/m³ for PM1, PM2.5, PM4 and PM10 (same as SEN65)
PM precision (PM1, PM2.5): ±(5 µg/m³ + 5 % of reading) up to 100 µg/m³, then ±10 %
VOC and NOx index range: 1–500
Operating range: -10 to 50 °C absolute (recommended 10–40 °C)
Lifetime: >10 years
