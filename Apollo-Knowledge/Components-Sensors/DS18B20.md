Description: A digital 1-Wire temperature probe. Apollo sells optional waterproof flat-cable DS18B20 probes (short and long) for TEMP-1 and PLT-1 (soil temperature). The Apollo Breakout Module has a 1-Wire port for DS18B20 probes; in ESPHome use the One Wire component on GPIO6 with the Dallas temperature sensor. Site use cases include fridges, freezers and fish tanks.
Used in: [[PLT-1]], [[TEMP-1]], [[Apollo-Breakout-Module]]

Source: Maxim DS18B20 datasheet (REV 042208)
Measurement range: -55 to +125 °C
Accuracy: ±0.5 °C from -10 to +85 °C
Resolution: 9 to 12 bits, selectable
Interface: 1-Wire, with a unique 64-bit serial code
